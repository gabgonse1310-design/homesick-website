Safety

Child Safety Standards

Zero tolerance

Homesick has zero tolerance for child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSEA) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Prohibited content and conduct

Users must not create, upload, share, solicit, facilitate or promote content or conduct involving the sexual exploitation or abuse of children.

Enforcement and reporting

Homesick may remove prohibited content, restrict or terminate accounts, preserve relevant information where legally required, and report suspected child sexual exploitation or abuse to appropriate authorities or organizations when required by applicable law.
