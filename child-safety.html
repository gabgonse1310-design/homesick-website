Child Safety Standards

Homesick has zero tolerance for child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSEA) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Prohibited content and conduct

Users must not create, upload, share, solicit or facilitate content or conduct involving the sexual exploitation or abuse of children.

Response and reporting

We may remove prohibited content, restrict or terminate accounts, preserve relevant information where legally required, and report suspected child sexual exploitation or abuse to the appropriate authorities or organizations when required by applicable law.
